How routing works

Higher priority runs first. If that provider fails or is rate-limited, HOPE automatically tries your next enabled provider and finally the AI Hub fallback router. A provider's free tier or promotion can change, so AI Hub does not promise permanent free usage.

Permission & security rule

Every connection belongs to the signed-in user. Connecting does not grant unrelated requests automatic access. Sensitive app write actions require explicit confirmation where appropriate.

OAuth is shown only where a real supported authorization flow is configured. API-key providers use the encrypted credential vault. AI Hub never displays a fake Connected state.
